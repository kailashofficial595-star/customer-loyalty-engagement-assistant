Executive Summary

The Customer Loyalty and Engagement Assistant implements a robust, auditable hybrid architecture combining deterministic rule-based segmentation with policy-controlled grounded generation and multi-tier guardrails. It addresses all 10 Functional Requirements (FR-01 to FR-10) with zero hallucination, strict privacy masking, human-in-the-loop review, and a controlled mock delivery gate.
